## What is Caprae’s Mission?

Caprae’s mission is to help founders and operators build real businesses, not just close deals. The firm mixes operating experience, capital, and advisors, and it stays involved after the acquisition — Caprae treats M&A as a long journey, not a one-day close.

## How is Caprae Changing the ETA Space and Broader PE?

ETA is buy-and-run, not start-from-zero. The old path is slow: hire callers, buy three data tools, wait months for a real owner talk. A lot of PE still leans on shopped deals and financial engineering. Caprae goes the other way — off-market owners, a clear buy box, white-labeled outreach.

Search as a Service packages that: price, weekly rhythm, owner conversations without standing up a full in-house team. SaaSquatch makes the same idea self-serve for searchers who need an owner name, not another SDR filter.

The bigger shift is post-close. Caprae talks about control, not only capital. Value is made in the years after acquisition. Tools and operators do that work — not only a large fund staff.
